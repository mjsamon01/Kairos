# Kairos · Faith Planner

A pastel faith-inspired vision board, goal setter, weekly planner and to-do list.
Works offline and installs like a real app.

## Put it on GitHub Pages (free)

1. Sign in at github.com, then tap **+ → New repository**.
2. Name it `kairos`, set it to **Public**, and tap **Create repository**.
3. Tap **uploading an existing file**, then upload ALL of these files:
   `index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`.
   Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Build and deployment*, choose
   **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
5. Wait about a minute. Your app will be at
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/kairos/`

## Install it on your phone

- **iPhone (Safari):** open your link, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open your link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app**.

## Calendar

- In **Week**, tap the calendar icon on any item to add it to Google Calendar.
- Tap **Export this week (.ics)** or **Export all (.ics)** and open the file to add events to
  Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any other calendar.

## Your data

Everything is saved on your device only. Each phone or browser has its own copy.
Use **Back up data** / **Restore backup** at the bottom of the app to move it between devices.

## Updating later

Edit or replace `index.html` in your repo. Open the app twice to get the new version.
